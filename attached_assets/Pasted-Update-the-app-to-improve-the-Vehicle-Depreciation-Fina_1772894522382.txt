Update the app to improve the Vehicle / Depreciation / Finance logic so users can still get an estimate even if they do not know their exact WDV.

GOAL
Build a flexible vehicle ownership and depreciation section that supports:
- new vehicles
- previously claimed vehicles
- users who do not know their current WDV
- financed vehicles
- leased vehicles
- pro-rata timing based on purchase date and first business use date

IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULES
- Do not require exact WDV to unlock the estimator
- Let users choose whether the vehicle is new, previously claimed, or unknown
- If the user does not know WDV, allow an estimated depreciation position using purchase timing and prior-use inputs
- Mark all estimated values with an asterisk
- Finance details should feed into interest estimation only, not principal deduction
- All outputs must be labelled as estimates only, not tax advice

SECTION LOCATION
Add this inside the Vehicle Details area under two grouped cards:
1. Purchase, Ownership & Finance
2. Depreciation Details

--------------------------------------------------
DEPRECIATION ENGINE RULES
--------------------------------------------------

The depreciation engine must:
- use purchase date to determine where the vehicle is in the cycle
- support first-year pro-rata depreciation
- support year-on-year WDV roll forward
- use capped purchase value where relevant
- allow current WDV override
- allow prior claimed amount as an alternative starting point
- allow estimated WDV if exact values are unknown
- apply business use percentage to deductible depreciation
- update live when vehicle details, trip usage, or tax profile changes

--------------------------------------------------
READINESS LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------

Update deduction estimate readiness rules.

The deduction estimator should NOT require exact WDV if:
- purchase date exists
- purchase price exists
- vehicle history status exists
- enough information exists to estimate the depreciation cycle position

Readiness states:
- Locked = not enough vehicle/tax/trip data
- Partial estimate = some data missing, but estimate possible
- Active estimate = enough data for fuller estimate

Examples:
- If purchase date exists but WDV is unknown, allow partial estimate with asterisk
- If finance exists but annual interest is missing, keep estimate partial and flag finance as incomplete
- If depreciation inputs are estimated, keep deduction estimate in partial mode unless other key data is complete

SETTINGS INTEGRATION

- hide deduction values
- show locked or disabled state on summary cards

--------------------------------------------------
DISCLAIMERS
--------------------------------------------------

Always display one of these messages depending on state:

Locked:
Complete your profile to unlock deduction estimates.

Partial:
*Estimate based on current usage and available inputs only. This is not a full representation of your tax situation.

Active:
Planning estimate only. Final outcomes depend on your full tax position and should be reviewed by a registered tax agent.

Do not use wording that implies guaranteed refund, guaranteed claim, or tax advice.
