Tax Ruling TR 2021/1 · FY2024–25

Cents per km: $0.88/km (FY2024–25). No receipts needed, but capped at 5,000 km ($4,400 max). Simple — but leaves money on the table.

Logbook method: Business use % × actual vehicle expenses (fuel, rego, insurance, depreciation, etc). No kilometre cap. Requires a 12-week logbook — which WorkCar helps you build. For most tradies, this method returns a significantly larger deduction.

Estimates only. Final amounts depend on your individual tax circumstances. Confirm with a registered tax agent before lodging.
