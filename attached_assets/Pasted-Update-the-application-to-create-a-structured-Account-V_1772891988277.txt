Update the application to create a structured “Account & Vehicle” area that captures user details, vehicle information, tax estimate inputs, and app settings while keeping the interface simple and mobile-first.

GOAL
Provide all information required to:
- generate ATO-compliant logbook reports
- calculate estimated vehicle deductions
- allow tax agents to verify vehicle details
- support prorated depreciation calculations

NAVIGATION STRUCTURE

Add a new main section called:
Account

Inside the Account section create four panels:

1. My Details
2. Vehicle Details
3. Tax Estimate Profile
4. Settings

Keep the UI minimal and mobile-first with large touch targets.

TAX CALCULATION LOGIC

Use Australian resident tax rates:

0 – 18,200 = 0%
18,201 – 45,000 = 16%
45,001 – 135,000 = 30%
135,001 – 190,000 = 37%
190,001+ = 45%

Apply Medicare levy at 2%.

Flag possible Medicare levy surcharge if:

income exceeds threshold
AND
private hospital cover is "No".

EXPENSE SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Vehicle details must integrate with the Expenses module.

Fuel estimation must use:

Business KMs
Vehicle Fuel Consumption
Current Average Fuel Price

Formula:

Fuel Estimate =
Business KMs × Fuel Consumption ÷ 100 × Fuel Price

Mark all fuel estimates with an asterisk.

Example display:

Fuel Estimate: $1,482*

*Estimated using recorded business kilometres and current average fuel price. Final values may be adjusted by your tax agent.
